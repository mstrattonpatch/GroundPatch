Reference point

The Town Centre point or station entrance sites are measured against — per Housing SEPP Sch 10, click the specific point on the map (e.g. the mapped Town Centre boundary edge, or the station entrance).

LMR settings

Inner area (≤400m) = 2.2:1 FSR, Outer area (400–800m) = 1.5:1 FSR, per Housing SEPP s180. Arterial roads = OSM primary/secondary/trunk ways (a proxy — supply a TfNSW classified-roads service URL if you need the authoritative dataset). Restricted scenario only allows crossing an arterial at a tagged pedestrian crossing or traffic signal.
